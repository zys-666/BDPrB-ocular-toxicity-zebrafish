# 06_wetlab_stats — not provided

The manuscript reports statistics for Figures 1, 3 and 6 that have no script in
this release:

* Figure 1B-M  behaviour and morphometry, n = 15 per group (3 tanks x 5 fish)
* Figure 1N-R  retinal layer thickness, n = 3 per group
* Figure 3B-R  ELISA and RT-qPCR, n = 9 per group
* Figure 6B-J  recovery experiment, n = 9 (behaviour) and n = 3 (assays) per group

Methods 2.11 specifies nested one-way ANOVA with tank as a random factor followed
by Tukey HSD, with gene expression normalised by 2^-ddCt to the control mean and
behavioural indices expressed relative to the control mean.

These analyses were performed in SPSS v27 and are available from the corresponding
author on reasonable request.
